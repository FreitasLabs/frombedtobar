# From Bed to Bar — Editorial Review Markup

## How to Use This Worksheet

This document identifies wording for Stephanie to review. It does not supply replacement copy and does not authorize an edit.

Categories:

- **Voice** — may not sound like Stephanie.
- **Clarity** — a reader may misunderstand.
- **Freshness** — seasonal or dated wording may age.
- **Trust** — requires owner verification or support.
- **Opportunity** — could become more personal.

Priorities are assigned across the whole package. “Direction” describes the decision to make, not replacement wording. Repository line references are included for location only.
