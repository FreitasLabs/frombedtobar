# From Bed to Bar — Editorial Governance

## Purpose

This handbook governs future editorial work. It does not authorize changes by itself. Public copy changes require owner approval, and personal details must remain truthful to Stephanie's experience.

## Brand Voice

- Use first person whenever the statement is truthfully Stephanie's.
- Keep the voice warm, practical, personal, specific, family-centered, and calmly confident.
- Prefer concrete observations and lived details over broad lifestyle language.
- Preserve uncertainty when Stephanie is still learning or experimenting.
- Avoid generic AI language, inflated claims, repeated slogans, unsupported superlatives, and invented emotion.
- Never invent personal stories, family details, expertise, testing, results, or endorsements.

## Seasonal Writing

### Evergreen guidance

Evergreen guidance remains useful without a current date. It may describe general growing principles, techniques, or seasonal patterns, but should state the region, climate, or growing-zone limits whenever timing varies by place.

### Seasonal guidance

Seasonal guidance tells readers what is timely during a defined window. It must identify the applicable region or zone and display a seasonal review date. Phrases such as “right now,” “this week,” “today,” “current,” “peak now,” and “coming soon” may be used only when:

1. the statement is true on the publication date;
2. the relevant place or climate is clear;
3. a visible review or expiration date is present; and
4. an owner is responsible for reviewing or retiring it.

### Journal entries

Journal entries preserve a dated moment in the garden or home. They should display their publication date and may retain time-specific observations because the date supplies context. They should not be silently presented as current guidance later.

### Aging and expiration

- Seasonal modules need an owner, a visible seasonal review date, and a defined expiration or replacement date.
- When a review date passes, remove the “current” presentation, replace the claim with approved evergreen guidance, or publish a newly reviewed seasonal entry.
- “Coming soon” statements expire on their stated target date. If no target date exists, review them within 30 days.
- Undated “current” claims should not remain live.

## Publication Dates

### Published

The date when an editorial page or entry first becomes public. Preserve it when later revisions occur.

### Updated

The date of a meaningful editorial revision that changes reader-facing substance, accuracy, instructions, recommendations, or scope. Minor punctuation and formatting corrections do not qualify.

### Seasonal Review

The date on which an owner checked a seasonal claim against the stated region, zone, or season. It is separate from Published and Updated.

### `dateModified`

Use `dateModified` only when a meaningful editorial change is publicly released. Its value must agree with the visible Updated date when one is shown.

Technical fixes—including accessibility, performance, redirects, markup repair, image optimization, and code-only changes—do not count as editorial updates and must not advance visible editorial dates or `dateModified`.

## Authorship

- Preserve current authorship exactly as it exists until the owners approve a separate authorship decision.
- Do not assign new authors, infer an author, or convert organizational attribution to a person.
- Do not add reviewer credits, credentials, or endorsements without documented approval from that person.
- Future personal copy must be confirmed by Stephanie before publication.

## Future Content

### Recipes

- Record the source, test status, substitutions tested, and owner approval before publication.
- Keep ingredients, method, yield, visible claims, and structured data consistent.
- Distinguish editorial authorship from technical or safety review.

### Guides

- Separate lived experience from general guidance.
- Identify region, climate, or zone when advice is not universal.
- Keep claims specific, supportable, and within the owner's experience.

### Blog posts

- Treat posts as dated journal or editorial entries.
- Display Published and, when materially revised, Updated.
- Keep dated observations dated rather than presenting them as permanently current.

### Collections

- Collection introductions and filters must describe the items actually present.
- Time-sensitive labels require the same region, review-date, and expiration controls as other seasonal modules.
- Do not imply freshness, testing, or completeness that has not been verified.

### Newsletter

- Promise only the cadence and content the owners can consistently deliver.
- Seasonal claims in newsletter promotion need the same place and time context as website claims.
- Consent, privacy, account settings, and subscriber-data language remain governed by the separate privacy workflow.
